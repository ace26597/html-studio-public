A little ocean science

Tides, in plain words

The Moon’s gravity pulls more strongly on the side of Earth nearest to it than on the far side. Together with the motion of Earth and the Moon, this creates two broad bulges of ocean water on opposite sides of Earth. [1] As Earth turns through these bulges, most coasts get roughly two high tides a day. Not every coast follows this pattern. [2]

Three terms to know

TermMeaning
Spring tideA bigger gap between high and low tide, around a new or full moon. [3]
Neap tideA smaller gap between high and low tide, around a half moon. [3]
Tidal rangeThe difference in water height between high tide and low tide. [4]
