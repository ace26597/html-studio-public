What is not to scale

Distances and speeds are true to scale; sizes are not.
  • Planet and Sun sizes are exaggerated. At true scale against these orbits the planets would be invisible specks. Here the Sun is drawn smaller than its true size relative to the orbits and the planets much larger. Planets keep their size order, but the differences between them are compressed.
  • The Moon's orbit is drawn roughly 35 times farther from Earth than the planets' distance scale would put it, so that it can be told apart from Earth. Its period (27.3 days) is real.
  • Orbits are drawn as circles in one flat plane. Real orbits are slightly elliptical and tilted; distances use the fact sheet's mean distance from the Sun.
  • Starting positions are arbitrary (not today's date). Planet colours are simplified, with no surface detail, axial tilt or day/night spin.
